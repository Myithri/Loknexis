Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a service? ▼
Open Booking, enter your requirement and location, then compare and select a listed worker.
Can I choose my own worker? ▼
Yes. The demo shows matching worker profiles and sample ratings so you can choose.
Can I upload a problem photo? ▼
Yes. Open AI Assistant and upload an image. Actual AI diagnosis requires a connected AI service.
How do I rate a worker? ▼
Open Ratings & Reviews after a service and submit your feedback.
